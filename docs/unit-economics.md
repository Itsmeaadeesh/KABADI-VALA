# Kabadiwala Connect — Unit Economics & Platform Sustainability

**Smart India Hackathon Problem Statement ID: 26229**  
**"Kabadiwala Connect – Bringing the Informal Collector into the Formal Recycling Chain"**

---

## 1. The Value Leakage Problem

In the status quo informal recycling hierarchy:

```text
Informal Collector (Kabadiwala)
         │  Receives ~₹4,200 (Low bargaining power, arbitrary cash cut)
         ▼
Tier 1 Itinerant Aggregator / Scrap Yard
         │  Margin ~₹500
         ▼
Tier 2 Regional Scrap Wholesaler
         │  Margin ~₹550 (Hazardous rudimentary sorting, burning)
         ▼
Illegal Dismantler / Export Smuggler
         ▼
Final Extracted Value: High environmental & human health degradation
```

By disintermediating informal speculative middlemen and directly linking collectors to formal CPCB-authorized facilities, **Kabadiwala Connect creates an economic surplus that is shared between the informal collector and formal recycler**.

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
```

## 4. Formalization Benefits Beyond Direct Payout

1. **Digital Khata (Verified Income Record)**: Enables formal micro-credit eligibility and bank inclusion.
2. **Health Risk Mitigation**: Eliminates toxic emissions and chemical exposure at the source.
3. **Strategic Mineral Security**: Increases domestic retention of strategic minerals (Lithium, Neodymium, Cobalt, Copper).
