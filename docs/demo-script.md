# Kabadiwala Connect — 3-Minute SIH Jury Demo Script

**Smart India Hackathon Problem Statement ID: 26229**  
**"Kabadiwala Connect – Bringing the Informal Collector into the Formal Recycling Chain"**  
Issued by: **Ministry of Mines (MoM) / JNARDDC**

---

## Jury Presentation Pitch (Time: 3 Minutes)

### Minute 0:00 – 0:30: Problem & Opening
> *"Respected Jury Members, in India today over 90% of e-waste is processed through the informal sector. Kabadiwalas are exposed to hazardous fumes and cheated on rates by middlemen, while our nation loses tons of critical minerals like Copper, Lithium, and Cobalt. We present **Kabadiwala Connect**—a production-grade digital bridge connecting informal collectors directly to CPCB-authorized recyclers and Ministry of Mines oversight."*

---

### Minute 0:30 – 1:30: Live Collector Experience
1. **Login**: 
   - Click **🎬 Demo Mode: Collector (Ramesh)**.
   - Note the **360px mobile view**, high contrast, large icons, and permanent language toggle (Hindi / Marathi / English).
2. **Vernacular & Voice**:
   - Tap language toggle to **हिंदी**.
   - Tap the 🔊 **Listen** button on today's PCB rate card: hear natural colloquial speech (`"High Grade PCB ka aaj ka rate ₹410 per kilogram hai"`).
3. **Lot Creation with AI**:
   - Click **📸 SELL E-WASTE**.
   - Pick or take a photo of a Motherboard.
   - Watch the **AI Classifier**: Identifies *"Motherboard / PCB — 94% Confidence (High Grade)"*.
   - View the **Critical Mineral Indicator**: Purple badge warning *"Contains Copper (20%), Gold (250g/t), Tantalum. Do NOT burn or acid-leach!"*
4. **Weight & Instant Pricing**:
   - Use the simple stepper: click `[+5 KG]` twice + `[+1 KG]` + `[-1 KG]` -> **12.5 KG**.
   - Instant Estimator calculates: `12.5 KG × ₹420/KG = ₹5,250`.
   - Submit lot: Generates unique QR manifest: `LOT-2026-MH-4821`.
   - Toggle offline mode to show: `🟠 Offline — Saved Locally` updates without data loss.

---

### Minute 1:30 – 2:15: Authorized Recycler Handover
1. **Recycler View**:
   - Switch to **Recycler (GreenCycle Recycling, Mumbai)** via the top Demo Bar.
   - The newly created lot immediately appears in the **Incoming Lots** queue!
2. **Matching & Logistics**:
   - Note our 5-factor smart matching score (Authorization 30%, Material 25%, Rate 20%, Distance 15%, Pickup 10%).
   - Click **Schedule Pickup** or **Verify Handover**.
3. **Verification & Digital Manifest**:
   - Enter Lot ID `LOT-2026-MH-4821` (or scan QR).
   - Recycler verifies weight on calibrated scale: enter `12.2 KG`.
   - Adjusted final price: `₹5,124`.
   - Click **Confirm Handover & Pay via UPI**.
   - Instant digital receipt issued with reference ID `UPI-982173`.

---

### Minute 2:15 – 3:00: Khata Passbook & Government Oversight
1. **Collector Khata**:
   - Switch back to Collector.
   - Open **📒 Mera Khata**: `₹5,124` is recorded as verified paid income.
   - Download the official Income Statement.
2. **Ministry of Mines / JNARDDC Dashboard**:
   - Switch to **Admin (Ministry of Mines / JNARDDC)**.
   - Showcase national & Maharashtra district heatmaps.
   - View real-time **Critical Mineral Recovery**:
     - Copper: 92.4 tonnes
     - Lithium: 4.8 tonnes
     - Cobalt: 1.7 tonnes
     - Neodymium: 820 kg
     - Gold: 21.4 kg
   - Open **Unit Economics Calculator**: Show how disintermediating middlemen adds **+₹1,050 net income (+25%)** per lot to the grassroots collector while keeping the platform self-sustaining through recycler transaction fees and EPR credits.

> *"With Kabadiwala Connect, every discarded circuit board is safely channeled, the kabadiwala earns a dignified living with a digital footprint, and India secures its critical mineral sovereignty. Thank you!"*
